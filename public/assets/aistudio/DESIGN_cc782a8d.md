## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, dark-mode computational aesthetic crafted for elite football biomechanists, performance analysts, and sports medicine directors. It conveys surgical precision, athletic velocity, and algorithmic intelligence.

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Athletic Cybernetics & High-Precision HUD.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep stadium night immersion, high-contrast luminous data vis, and structured telemetry cards reminiscent of aerospace flight recorders and Formula 1 pit-wall monitors.
- **Personality:** Analytical, relentless, authoritative, scientific, and sharp.
- **Audience Impact:** The interface inspires supreme confidence in tracking metrics, injury risk vectors, joint angular velocity, and ball-striking kinematics without visual fatigue during high-intensity analytical sessions.

## Colors

The palette leverages an obsidian deep-space canvas paired with vivid, laser-focused luminescent functional accents. 

### Canvas & Surface Structure
- **Canvas Base:** `#0B0F17` (Pitch Obsidian) provides the deepest viewport layer, eliminating light glare and magnifying 3D wireframes.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Shells):** `#111827` delivers clear containment for sidebars, top navigations, and dock bars.
- **Surface Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** `#1F2937` creates visual grouping for kinematic charts, player profiles, and event logs.
- **Surface Level 3 (Inputs & Hover states):** `#374151` ensures distinct affordance for interaction states.

### Data & Accent Logic
- **Primary Peak Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied exclusively to optimal performance metrics, peak power benchmarks, optimal joint alignment ranges, and primary interactive trigger states.
- **Kinematic Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Governs computer vision tracking vectors, spatial mesh nodes, joint degree calipers, trajectory lines, and telemetry feeds.
- **Telemetry Caution Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Warns of asymmetric mechanical load, fatigue thresholds, and elevated torsion risks.
- **Biomechanical Alert Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Flags hyperextension, high injury probability, system errors, and critical motion alerts.
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:**
  - High-Emphasis Text: `#F9FAFB`
  - Medium-Emphasis / Subheaders: `#9CA3AF`
  - Low-Emphasis / Micro-telemetry: `#6B7280`
  - Structural Ghost Borders: `#1F2937` / `#374151`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is divided into three distinct functional roles:
- **Display & Section Titles (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers geometric authority and an athletic, cutting-edge technical tone. Display titles use tighter tracking to mimic digital timing consoles.
- **Narrative & UI Flow (`Inter`):** Selected for its neutral clarity, tall x-height, and fatigue-free legibility in high-density data tables and analytical breakdowns.
- **Telemetry, Timestamps & Kinematic Readouts (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used for all numerical readings (e.g., `42.8°`, `3.42 m/s²`, `98.2 Hz`, frame counts, and spatial coordinates) to avoid tabular jitter and preserve vertical alignment across data columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt dense telemetry grid designed for multi-stream spatial analysis, dual-axis graphs, and synchronized 60fps/120fps video tracking feeds.

### Structural Flow
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic grid with a persistent narrow tool telemetry sidebar (72px collapsed / 240px expanded), an inspection stage for video/spatial 3D tracking, and a synchronized modular panel for biomechanical parameters.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout. Analytical parameters drop into collapsible bottom drawers or side tabs.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with consolidated stacked metrics, sticky scrubber controls, and card carousels.

### Spacing Density Principles
- Module headers maintain a tight `space-sm` gap to their relative chart components to enforce immediate data grouping.
- High-density telemetry blocks (e.g., knee flexion angle, hip rotation, impulse velocity) use `space-xs` between label and value.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, but on **tonal stratification, hairline borders, and subtle luminous backdrops**.

### Depth Layers
- **Ground Floor (Pitch Black):** `#0B0F17` for viewport backplates and canvas stages.
- **Layer 1 (Card Matrix):** `#111827` bordered with a 1px crisp rule of `#1F2937`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays & Video Tooltips):** `#1F2937` with 1px border `#374151` and a soft ambient blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with 85% opacity).
- **Layer 3 (Modal Alerts & Calibration Overlays):** `#111827` accompanied by a localized neon luminescence around active focal elements (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` or `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`).

### Hairline Rules & Contrast
- Cards and metric splitters utilize sharp 1px borders rather than diffusion, reinforcing the feeling of precision optics and lab equipment calibration lines.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a controlled, engineered geometry (`roundedness: 1` — base radius of `4px`, with card/container maximums at `8px` and `12px`).

- **Base Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Tiles):** `4px` (`rounded-sm`). Crisp, technical, and space-efficient.
- **Containers & Video Playback Viewports:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`). Provides containment without sacrificing technical sharpness.
- **Floating Modals & Spatial Canvas Overlays:** `12px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Telemetry Badges & Frame Indicators:** `2px` to `4px`. Pill shapes are avoided for structural data; pills are reserved strictly for high-visibility status indicators (e.g., "LIVE TRACKING" or "AI INFERENCE READY").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Trigger:** Background `#10B981`, foreground `#0B0F17`, font `Inter` SemiBold (14px). Hover state transitions to a luminous green glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Telemetry Action:** Background transparent, 1px border `#06B6D4`, text `#06B6D4`. Hover fills with `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Background `#1F2937`, text `#F9FAFB`, border 1px `#374151`.

### Data Cards & Metric Tiles
- **Structure:** Surface `#111827`, border 1px solid `#1F2937`.
- **Header:** Upper label in `JetBrains Mono` uppercase (`label-sm`), color `#9CA3AF`.
- **Metric Core:** Dynamic value in `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono` bold, paired with an inline vector delta (`+14.2%` in `#10B981` or `-3.1°` in `#EF4444`).
- **Accent Indicator:** Top-edge or left-edge 2px indicator bar colored according to metric status (Cyan for kinematic, Green for peak output, Amber for load limit).

### Badges & Technical Chips
- **Design:** Micro-height (20px to 24px), background `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`, border 1px solid `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`, text `#06B6D4`, font `JetBrains Mono` (10px, uppercase).
- **Status Dots:** 6px glowing indicator dot preceding text (pulsing emerald for active recording/inference).

### Input Fields & Scrubbers
- **Inputs:** Dark field `#0B0F17`, 1px border `#374151`, focus ring `#10B981` with zero blur offset. Text color `#F9FAFB`. Placeholder color `#6B7280`.
- **Biomechanical Video Timeline Scrubber:** Track `#1F2937`, buffered region `#374151`, active playhead hairline in `#06B6D4` with keyframe kinematic markers indicated by miniature diamond nodes (`#10B981` and `#F59E0B`).

### Kinematic & Radar Visualizations
- **Radar Charts:** Web axes rendered in `#374151` with `#06B6D4` polygon overlays at 20% opacity and 1.5px solid stroke.
- **Skeleton / 3D Joint Overlay:** Joint nodes rendered as 4px concentric rings in `#06B6D4` with vectors rendered in 2px semi-transparent cyan lines; load peaks transition instantly into `#10B981` or `#EF4444`.
